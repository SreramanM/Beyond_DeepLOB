**ReadMe:**
We introduce three new deep learning models for mid-price direction prediction on the FI-2010 NoAuction dataset at horizon k=100: PVSLOB (price-volume structured transformer with alternating feature- and time-dimension attention), VGLOB (a FiLM-gated backbone conditioned on spread and order book imbalance), and SAL (sparse attention LOB using top-k masked multi-head attention over the temporal sequence). All models build on a compact DeepLOB-inspired convolutional backbone adapted for 144 features, are trained on CF_7 with a 99-window embargo, and evaluated on CF_8+CF_9 across two normalizations (DecPre and ZScore), investigating whether structured attention or microstructure-conditioned gating improves predictive accuracy over the convolutional baseline.
